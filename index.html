Where Does the Energy Go?

The digital infrastructure consumes 500-700 TWh annually — roughly equivalent to the entire electricity consumption of France and Germany combined.

Data Centers: 240-340 TWh (storage, processing, cooling)

Networks: 260-360 TWh (mobile networks ~2/3 of total)

Crypto Mining: 110 TWh (0.4% of global demand)

Geography Impact

Not All Data Centers Are Equal

A data center in Sweden running on 98% clean energy has a completely different carbon footprint than one in a coal-heavy grid. Location is everything.

Sweden: ~5 gCO₂/kWh — among the cleanest grids in the world.

India: ~700 gCO₂/kWh — heavily reliant on coal power.

140x difference! The same computation in India produces 140x more CO₂ than in Sweden.
