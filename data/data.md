## Google Cloud CFE (Carbon Free Energy)
| Folder | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| `yearly Carbon free energy for Google Cloud regions/` | Annual CFE % and grid carbon intensity by region (2019–2024) |

### Key Metrics
- **CFE %** – Hourly average of carbon-free energy consumed (includes grid + Google's renewable investments)
- **Grid Carbon Intensity (gCO₂eq/kWh)** – Emissions per unit of grid electricity
- **PUE** – Power Usage Effectiveness (1.0 = perfect efficiency)
